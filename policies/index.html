Privacy & Terms

The short version of how we handle your information and your order.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you send an order request, contact form, track-order request or newsletter sign-up, we receive the details you type in: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order notes.

How we use it

We use these details to reply to you, confirm and fulfil your order, arrange delivery and — if you opt in — send occasional updates about new pieces. We don't sell or rent your information.

Payment information

This website does not collect card numbers or process payments. Payment is arranged separately after your order is confirmed.

Cookies and local storage

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Your cart and wishlist are stored in your browser's local storage so they survive a page refresh. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Who processes your data

Form submissions are delivered through Netlify, which hosts this site. Netlify processes submissions on our behalf and may store them outside Nigeria. Fonts and icons are loaded from Google Fonts and Cloudflare.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you've sent us by emailing info@litty.com. You can unsubscribe from newsletter emails at any time by replying to any email.

Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

Orders are requests

Submitting an order on this site is a request, not a completed purchase. We'll confirm availability, shipping and the final total by email or WhatsApp before any payment is made.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in Nigerian Naira (₦) and may change without notice. Shipping is free on orders over ₦75,000; a flat fee of ₦7,500 applies below that. Payment is agreed after confirmation.

Handmade pieces

Every piece is handmade, so colour, texture, size and finish can vary slightly from photographs. These variations are part of the product, not defects, and pieces are generally one of a kind.

Delivery

We'll share an expected dispatch date when we confirm your order. Delivery times depend on your location and the courier. Risk passes to you on delivery.

Returns

Please see the returns section of our FAQ. Faulty or incorrect pieces should be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos so we can help.

Content

The Litty name, logo, photography and copy on this site belong to Litty and may not be reused without permission.

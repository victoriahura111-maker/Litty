Help

FAQ, Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know before and after you order.

Shipping

What to expect when your piece is on its way

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on orders over ₦75,000. Below that, a flat shipping fee of ₦7,500 applies. The exact amount is shown in your cart and again on your order request.

Do you ship internationally?

We're based in Nigeria and can quote international delivery on request. Choose "Other" at checkout or send us a message with your address before ordering.

How long does delivery take?

Dispatch times vary because each piece is made and packed by hand. When we confirm your order we'll give you an expected dispatch date and delivery window.

How do I get a tracking number?

Once your order is dispatched we'll send the tracking details by email or WhatsApp. You can also request a status update.

Returns

Handmade pieces, handled with care

What if my piece arrives damaged?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the piece and the packaging. We'll arrange a replacement or refund where possible.

Can I return a piece I've changed my mind about?

Pieces must be unused and in their original packaging. Message us before sending anything back so we can confirm whether a return is possible and where to send it.

What about small differences in colour or shape?

Those are part of buying handmade. Slight variations in glaze, texture and size are expected and aren't considered faults.

Who pays return shipping?

If the piece is faulty or not what you ordered, we'll cover return shipping. For change-of-mind returns, the cost is yours unless we agree otherwise.

Ordering

Questions we get asked a lot

How do I place an order?

Add your pieces to the cart and send an order request with your delivery details. Prefer to talk first? Use the contact form or WhatsApp.

Do I pay online?

No card details are collected on this site. We confirm stock, shipping and the total first, then agree payment by bank transfer, pay on delivery or WhatsApp.

Are the pieces really one of a kind?

Yes. Every piece is handmade, so the exact piece can't be remade. If you'd like something similar, we can discuss a custom order.

Can I request a custom piece?

Absolutely. Send a message with your idea, size, colours and timeline, and we'll tell you what's possible.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes, for retailers and resellers. Contact us with quantities and delivery details for a quote.

How should I care for my piece?

Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid harsh cleaners, prolonged soaking and drops — clay is durable but not indestructible.

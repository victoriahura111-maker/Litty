Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tracking

How do I track my order?

Send your order number and email using the form above. We'll reply with the latest status of your shipment.

When will my order ship?

Because pieces are made and packed by hand, dispatch times vary. We'll confirm the expected dispatch date when we approve your order.

What if my tracking number isn't working?

Tracking information can take 24–48 hours to update after dispatch. If it's still not working, contact us and we'll chase it up.

Can I change my shipping address?

Contact us as soon as possible. We can change the address only before the order is dispatched.
